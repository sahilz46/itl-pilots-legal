Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

In short. ITL Pilots is a work app for ITL World's drivers and dispatch office. It shares a driver's location with the office only while the driver is on duty, and that sharing stops when they end their day. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell personal data.

Who we are

ITL Pilots is provided by I.T.L Tourism & Travel (L.L.C) ("ITL World", "we"), Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We are responsible for the personal data processed through the app. You can reach us about privacy at sahil.m@miceminds.com.

Who uses the app

The app is for ITL World staff only: drivers, and the dispatch office that assigns their work. Accounts are created by the company. There is no public sign-up, and the app is not intended for anyone under 18.

What we collect, and why

DataWhat it isWhy we use it
Account detailsName, work email, phone number, role, and a profile photo if you add one.To sign you in, show who you are to your colleagues and the office, and let the office contact you.
LocationYour phone's precise location while you are on duty, including when the app is closed or not in use. It stops when you end your day or sign out.So the office can see where drivers are, assign the nearest driver to a job, and measure distance driven.
Work recordsTasks assigned to you, when you start, finish or could not complete them, an optional finish photo, your on-duty, break and off-duty times, and overtime.To run the day's work and to produce reports on deliveries, punctuality and hours worked.
Vehicle recordsWhich company car you take, odometer readings, fuel, fines, problem reports, and photos of the car.To manage the company's cars, their servicing and their costs.
MessagesText, photos, short videos, voice notes, documents and shared locations you send in the app.So drivers and the office can talk about the work. Messages between two drivers are private to them and cannot be read by the office in the app.
Device informationA notification token for your phone, and whether location access is allowed.To send you notifications about your work, and to tell the office why your position might be missing.

The camera, microphone and photo library are only used when you choose to take a photo, record a voice note or a video, or pick a file to send.

Who can see it

  • The dispatch office of your company sees your account details, work and vehicle records, your messages with the office, and your location while you are on duty or on a break. It does not see your location when you are off duty.
  • Your colleagues see only your name, photo and whether you are available, and the messages you send them.
  • Nobody outside the company, apart from the service providers below, who process data only to run the app.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the app's data and files and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Expo delivers notifications to your phone, through Apple (iPhone) and Google (Android) notification services.
  • Google Maps Platform draws maps on Android, suggests places when the office types an address, and turns a driver's position into a street name for the office. Only the search text or the position is sent, not your name.

We do not use advertising or analytics services.

How long we keep it

  • Detailed location history: 45 days. A daily total of distance driven is kept with your work records.
  • Messages: 180 days. Files attached to messages may be kept until the company removes them.
  • Notifications in the app: 90 days.
  • The activity log of shifts and task changes: 365 days.
  • Tasks, vehicle records and reports: for as long as the company needs them for its operations and legal obligations.

When a driver leaves, their sign-in is switched off straight away. Their past work records stay with the company so reports remain correct, unless deletion is requested as described below.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn location access off in your phone's settings at any time. The office will then not see where you are.
  • You can correct your name, phone number and photo in the app's settings.
  • Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, and other laws that apply to you, you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. See how to ask for your account and data to be deleted.

How we protect it

All data travels over encrypted connections. Each person can only reach the data their role and their own company allows; this is enforced by the database itself. Photos, voice notes and files are kept in private storage and opened through short-lived links. Your sign-in is stored in your phone's secure storage and is cleared when you sign out.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
