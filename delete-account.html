Delete your account and data

Effective 20 October 2026

ITL Pilots accounts are created by ITL World for its staff. You can ask for your account and personal data to be deleted at any time, including after you have uninstalled the app.

How to ask

Email sahil.m@miceminds.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject ITL Pilots account deletion. You can also ask your dispatch office in person.

We confirm the request with you, then complete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and account.
  • Your name, phone number and profile photo.
  • Your location history and your notification token.
  • Messages you sent, and the photos, videos, voice notes and files attached to them.

What may be kept

Records the company is legally required to keep, or needs for its accounts, such as completed tasks, traffic fines and vehicle records, may be kept for as long as that obligation lasts. Where we keep them, your name is removed or replaced wherever the law allows.

When you leave the company, your sign-in is switched off straight away, even before a deletion request.
